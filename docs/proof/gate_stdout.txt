# Refusal gate report

Generated by `python -m raglens.eval.train_gate` in 1.6s with embedder `hash-384 (fallback)`. Every number below comes from that run.

- Training data (dev only): 79 answerable and 16 unanswerable questions from `data/eval/qa.jsonl`, `data/eval/banking_dev.jsonl`
- Held-out test: `data/eval/banking_test.jsonl` (32 questions, never used for training or threshold selection)
- Costs: answering an unanswerable question = 3, refusing an answerable one = 1
- Chosen threshold on P(answerable): **0.46**
